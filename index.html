the vision

A rave at breakfast

A sober, all-ages sunrise rave at Jay's house: a DJ, great speakers, costumes, props, bubble guns, art, vendors and a lot of joy.

Kids dance next to their parents. Grandparents welcome. Coffee instead of cocktails, egg tacos instead of a bar tab.

the lineup

What's on

  • DJ set

  • Egg taco bar

    Scrambled eggs, chorizo, papas, beans, a full salsa bar. Veggie friendly.

  • Sunrise bar

    Cold brew, pineapple lemonade, spa water.

  • Bubble station

    Giant bubbles and bubble guns.

  • Kids' zone

    Crafts, chalk, face gems.

  • Art & vendors

    Live painting, local makers, henna and temporary tattoos.

  • Costume floor

    Props bin at the door. Grab a flower crown.

  • Lounge

    Couches and shade when you need a breather.

FAQ

Is it really at 9am?+

Yes. Doors at 9, last song at 12:45.

Can I bring my kids?+

Please. There's a kids' zone.

Do I have to wear a costume?+

No, but you'll wish you had.

What should I bring?+

Sunscreen, a hat, a water bottle. Food and drinks are covered.

What if it rains?+

It moves under the garage and tents. Same time.

Where do I park?+

Street parking is limited. Rideshare or carpool.

Where is it exactly?+

Jay's house in Los Angeles. The address comes in your RSVP confirmation email.

What does Bani Adam mean?+

“Children of Adam”: all of us. It's the opening of a poem by Saadi about how every person is part of one body.
